Safeguards

How your money is protected

You don't have to take the operator's word for it. The smart contract has two checks built in: every monthly income report must pass an automatic check, and the money paid for Sol Coins is released only as the project reaches approved milestones.

DEMOSample records and test money. These safeguards show how the checks work. They can't prove that real electricity was produced or real work was done.

Who does what

Four separate parties take part. No single wallet can both report the figures and approve them.

Solar operator

Runs the rooftop project. Publishes a report every month and submits records for each funding stage.

Checking service

Reviews each month's records and digitally signs the figures. The contract only accepts reports it has signed.

Milestone reviewer

Reads the operator's records for each funding stage, then approves the stage or asks for changes.

You, the investor

Nothing to do here. Follow progress any time, no wallet needed, and claim your income on My Sol Coins.

1
Safeguard 1

Every monthly report is checked automatically

Income reaches shareholders only through a monthly report. Before the contract accepts one, a separate checking service must sign its figures, and the operator must deposit exactly the income those figures add up to.

  1. Step 1

    The month ends

    The operator gathers the month's meter readings, operating costs and repair reserve.

  2. Step 2

    The records are checked

    The checking service compares the records and signs the figures. A signature is valid for one hour.

  3. Step 3

    Report plus payment

    The operator publishes the signed figures and deposits the income they add up to, to the rupiah.

  4. Step 4

    The contract verifies

    A wrong signature, month or amount is rejected. Once accepted, shareholders can claim right away.

    Getting started

    Do I need to do anything on this page?

    No. As an investor, both safeguards run on their own. You can follow progress here at any time, even without connecting a wallet. Only the operator, the checking service and the milestone reviewer take actions.

    Why are the submit and approve buttons missing or greyed out?

    Every action belongs to one specific wallet. Only the operator wallet can submit records, and only the reviewer wallet can approve them. Everyone else sees progress, not buttons.

    In the local demo, use Try it as under Your part to switch between the operator and the reviewer. With MetaMask, switch to the matching account.

    What are the demo wallets?

    In the local demo, test accounts on your computer play each role: Alice and Budi are investors, plus the solar operator and the milestone reviewer. Switching between them lets you walk through the whole flow on your own. They only hold test ETH.

    Why can't I buy Sol Coins right now?

    Purchases pause when a monthly report is overdue or when the operator pauses them. Claiming income and sending Sol Coins keep working either way. Purchases reopen as soon as the overdue report is published.

    The operator wallet can never buy Sol Coins. Switch to an investor wallet from the wallet menu.

    It says milestone funding isn't active. Why?

    Milestones need the newest contract, which runs in the local blockchain demo started with npm start. The public Sepolia contract and the browser simulation don't include it, so sale proceeds there aren't locked by stage.

    Is any of this real money or a real project?

    No. The solar project is fictional, the records are labelled samples, and every payment uses test ETH, which has no value. Nothing here gives you legal ownership of anything.

    Automatic checks

    What exactly does the automatic check verify?

    Four things, all checked by the contract itself:

    • the checking service signed the figures,
    • they're for this project and the month that's due,
    • the signature hasn't expired, and
    • the operator's deposit equals the income the figures add up to: electricity sold, minus costs and the repair reserve.

    If any one of these fails, the report is rejected and nothing is paid out.

    Can the operator change the numbers after they've been checked?

    No. The signature covers every figure. Changing even one rupiah of generation, costs or reserve breaks it, and the contract refuses the report.

    Who runs the checking service? Could the operator check itself?

    The checking service is a separate wallet, fixed when the contract was created. The contract refuses to start if the checker and the operator are the same wallet. In the local demo, the checking service runs on your computer next to the test chain.

    Why does the operator send money with the report?

    That deposit is the income. The moment the report is accepted, the contract splits it equally across all 1,000 Sol Coins, so the published figures and the money paid out can never disagree.

    When is a report due, and what happens if it's late?

    Reporting for a month opens when that month ends, and the report is due 7 days later. Months must be reported in order, without skipping any.

    If the deadline passes, new purchases are blocked until the report is published. Claims and transfers keep working.

    What does "waiting to be claimed" mean?

    It's income that has been deposited but not yet claimed by shareholders. The contract keeps it separate from sale proceeds, so the operator can't withdraw it. Your share waits for you on My Sol Coins.

    I'm the operator and my check expired. What now?

    Signed figures are only valid for one hour, so an old approval can't be reused later. In the Operator lab, click Check monthly data again to get a fresh signature, then publish.

    Does a passed check prove the panels produced real electricity?

    No. It proves the figures match what the checking service signed and that the deposit matches the figures. A check is only as good as the records behind it, and in this demo those are sample data.

    Funding milestones

    Where does my money go when I buy Sol Coins?

    Into the smart contract, not the operator's wallet. It stays locked there and becomes available to the operator only as each funding stage is approved.

    How much is unlocked at each stage?

    30% after Equipment purchase, another 40% after Installation complete (70% in total), and the final 30% after System handover. The percentages apply to everything raised, including Sol Coins bought later.

    What does the reviewer check?

    The operator's records for the stage, such as purchase orders, inspection reports or handover documents. In this demo they're clearly labelled sample statements. Before deciding, the reviewer must confirm they've read the exact submission.

    What happens when the reviewer asks for changes?

    The stage stays locked, and the reviewer's note appears on it. The operator fixes the records and submits again; each new submission gets a new number. Nothing unlocks until a submission is approved.

    Does approving a stage send money to the operator?

    No. Approval only unlocks funding. The operator still has to withdraw it from the Operator lab, and every withdrawal is recorded on the chain.

    Can stages be skipped or approved out of order?

    No. The contract only accepts the next stage in line. Stage 2 can't be submitted until Stage 1 is approved, and so on.

    What if the reviewer stops responding or loses their wallet?

    Funding that hasn't been approved stays locked. This demo has no way to replace the reviewer or refund buyers. That's a known limit of the demo.

    Do milestones affect my monthly income?

    No. Milestones only control the money raised from Sol Coin sales. Monthly income from reports goes to shareholders separately, and you can claim it at any time.

    What is a record fingerprint? Are submissions private?

    A fingerprint is a short code calculated from the exact submitted text. If even one character changes, the fingerprint changes, so anyone can tell the reviewer approved exactly what was submitted. It doesn't prove who wrote the records.

    Submissions aren't private: the full text and its fingerprint are stored on the test chain for anyone to read.

    How Sol Invictus works

    From sunlight on a roof in Cikarang to income in your wallet, in five steps.

    1. 1→

      Sunlight

      The 10 kWp panels on a Cikarang rooftop turn sunlight into electricity, about 1,200 kWh a month.

    2. 2→

      Electricity sold

      The building pays for the power it uses. That payment is the project's income.

    3. 3→

      Monthly report

      The operator reports the kWh, takes out operating costs and a maintenance reserve, and deposits the rest.

    4. 4→

      Split onchain

      The smart contract divides the deposit equally across all 1,000 Sol Coins, including unsold ones held by the operator. Anyone can check it.

    5. 5

      Your wallet

      Your cut builds up with every report. Claim it to your wallet whenever you like.
