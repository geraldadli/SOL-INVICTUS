This hackathon prototype lets you buy, hold, and transfer shares in a fictional solar project, then claim simulated income.

No panels, meters, utilities, or fiat payments are connected. Tokens do not provide legal ownership.

The demo numbers

1,000 equal shares. Unsold units belong to the operator. Income is allocated to whoever holds the shares when the operator deposits it.

Rp1,000,000 = 0.001 test ETH is a presentation scale, not an exchange rate. Reports are simulated; blockchain records do not prove physical energy production.
